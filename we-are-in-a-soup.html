context

a community kitchen, and a website that recreates it.

in a world oversaturated with bad news and high accessibility to the worst happening everywhere, what we can really do is extend our arms to the people around us. so we ran a community kitchen with space to talk about issues around the world. the website is a recreation of the space we ran the project in.

design decisions

how do you bring a physical kitchen online?the website is a recreation of the space we ran the project in. why we chose to recreate it rather than design a new space is still to add.

what do you do after showing people the news?the cutscene shows the stats, then ends with an action: feed a friend / help a friend.

how does it spread?the fourth table leads to a page on how to build a community kitchen.

why does nobody win?nobody wins here. the work is the room.

what went wrong

the website is technically poorly structured and messy. there should be more guidelines, and the UX can be heavily improved. the site is under maintenance.

takeaways

  • the work is the room.
  • visitors roam. i hand over control.
  • people need guidelines even when they can roam. still to confirm.
